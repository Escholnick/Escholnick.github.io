Welcome to Outdoor Innovations, a mountain tech festival

Outdoor inovations is a new festival built around celebrating the beauty of the mountains and the technology we use to enjoy them. We are featuring local merchants and small businesses while also building a framework to attract big-name outdoor companies like Patagonia or Volcom.

Tickets are on sale now and will only increase in price, so get them before they do. If you wish to sell anything, a merchant booth slot is a must-have. We have discounts for locals in both catagories, so cost should be no issue.
